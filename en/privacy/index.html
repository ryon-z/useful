Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 / Last Revised: October 28, 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Client-Side Processing)

At GoModo US, we are deeply committed to your privacy. All of our online tools—including the Powerball and Mega Millions lottery tax calculator, the unit converter, and the restaurant tip splitter—execute entirely inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit, log, record, or store any of your inputs, financial numbers, or calculation results on our servers.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense and third-party advertising vendors to display advertisements when you visit our website.

Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Google Analytics & Web Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregated, anonymous traffic data (such as page views, browser types, and approximate geographic regions) to analyze performance and improve tool usability. Google Analytics employs cookies to collect standard internet log information. No personally identifiable information (PII) is transmitted to Google Analytics.

4. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. Because GoModo US does not collect, retain, or sell any consumer personal information, we inherently comply with all "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" mandates.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our team via email at support@gomodo.cloud.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time in response to operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
