Q. How are Powerball and Mega Millions taxes calculated?
The lottery agency automatically withholds 24% for federal taxes upfront. When filing your annual IRS return, multimillion-dollar prizes reach the top 37% federal tax bracket, requiring an additional ~13% payment. Our calculator factors in this total federal liability plus individual state and local taxes for all 50 states.
