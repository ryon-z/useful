How US Mortgage Payments Are Calculated (The Formula)

US fixed-rate mortgage payments are based on standard amortization mechanics. The monthly principal and interest payment is calculated using the standard annuity formula:

M = P × [ r(1 + r)n ] ÷ [ (1 + r)n - 1 ]
M: Monthly Payment (P&I)
P: Principal Loan Amount
r: Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12)
n: Total Payments (Years × 12)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What does PITI stand for?

PITI represents Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance. It encompasses the four core components of an escrowed monthly mortgage payment in the United States.

How does Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) work?

If your down payment is less than 20%, lenders require PMI to mitigate default risk. Annual PMI typically costs between 0.5% and 1.2% of the original loan balance. By federal law, you can request PMI cancellation once your loan principal reaches 80% of original value, and lenders must automatically cancel it at 78%.

Where do GoModo benchmark rates originate?

GoModo integrates weekly 30-Year Fixed Rate Mortgage benchmark averages published by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED MORTGAGE30US series) using automated serverless data pipelines.
