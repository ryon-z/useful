Mission & Purpose

Everyday Math & Finance,
Reimagined for Speed & Clarity.

GoModo US is a dedicated web utility studio designed to provide 100% free, private, and instant online calculators. We believe everyday domestic and financial calculations should never require app installations, subscriptions, or invasive data collection.

Our 3 Core Principles

100% Client-Side Privacy

All calculations run locally inside your browser sandbox. We never transmit, store, or sell any of your numbers, financial figures, or personal inputs.

Official Tax & NIST Standards

Our calculators strictly adhere to current IRS publication guidelines, 2026 federal brackets, 50-state tax laws, and NIST metric conversion factors.

Zero Bloat, Blazing Speed

Built without bloated multi-megabyte frontend frameworks. Loads instantaneously across desktop, iPhone, Android, and tablets.
