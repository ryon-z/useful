Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is the exact formula to convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?
Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature, multiply by 5, and divide by 9: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9. Conversely, to convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply by 9/5 (1.8) and add 32: °F = (°C × 1.8) + 32.
Q. Why does the US use Customary Units instead of the Metric System?
The US Customary system evolved from the British Imperial system inherited during colonial times. While the Metric Conversion Act was passed in 1975, voluntary conversion meant most American industries and public road signage maintained miles, pounds, and Fahrenheit due to the enormous cost of retooling national infrastructure.
Q. How many cups are in a US liquid pint, quart, and gallon?
In US liquid measurement: 1 cup = 8 fluid ounces. 1 pint = 2 cups (16 fl oz). 1 quart = 2 pints (4 cups, 32 fl oz). 1 gallon = 4 quarts (8 pints, 16 cups, 128 fl oz, or 3.785 Liters).
